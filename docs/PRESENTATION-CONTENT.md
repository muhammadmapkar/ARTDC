# Slide 11 — Prototype walkthrough and classroom setup

**Headline:** One AC concern, one transparent customer journey.

**Visual suggestion:** Four connected phone screenshots: symptom report; quote explanation and 65/100 score; garage comparison and Trust Passport; Quote Lock and repair timeline.

**On slide:**

1. Select the 2022 Toyota Corolla and report warm AC air.
2. Read preliminary guidance and the itemized AED 1,100 sample quotation.
3. Spot missing part and warranty details; generate a question for the mechanic.
4. Compare three fictional garages, inspect a Trust Passport and select one.
5. Authorize the AED 950 confirmed quote, decide on AED 300 of extra work, then view the repair record.

**Presenter note:** Reset through **Profile → Reset Demo** before class. Follow `docs/CLASSROOM-DEMO.md`, then show the printable Repair Audit Card. All AI guidance, reviews, verification indicators, tracking updates and prices are prototype examples.

# Slide 12 — Feature to pain point alignment matrix

| Pain point | Feature | Customer benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unclear mechanical terms | Guided symptoms and plain language guidance | Better questions for a qualified mechanic |
| Opaque quotation | Item explanations and 65/100 completeness score | Missing part category and warranty become visible |
| Difficult garage comparison | Sortable profiles and Trust Passport | Compare scope, part type, timing, warranty and cost |
| Surprise cost increases | Quote Lock decision history | Base and extra work receive separate recorded decisions |
| Poor repair documentation | Timeline and invoice in My Garage | Final scope and authorized amount remain easy to revisit |
