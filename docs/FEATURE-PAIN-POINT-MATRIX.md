# Feature to pain point alignment

These are design hypotheses for the INE 301 prototype. **[Insert teammate research findings and citations when supplied.]**

| Customer pain point | RepairLens feature | How it helps | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unfamiliar mechanical language | Guided symptom report and plain language guidance | Turns the AC concern into possibilities and questions without claiming a diagnosis | Report → Guidance |
| Confusing repair quotation | Item explanations and parts, labour, fees breakdown | Shows what the sample AED 1,100 charge contains | Analyze → Sample Quote |
| Missing quote details | 65/100 Repair Protection Score checklist | Highlights that part category and warranty are absent from the example | Quote → Protection |
| Not knowing what to ask | Generated example message | Provides wording to request written clarification before approval | Protection → Generate Questions |
| Price alone hides differences | Three garage cards with four sort controls | Places scope, part category, warranty, timing, sample rating and AED total together | Garages tab |
| Unclear trust indicators | Mechanic Trust Passport | Shows sample profile attributes while explicitly labelling verification as simulated | Garage card → Trust Passport |
| Unexpected cost increases | Quote Lock and additional work decision | Records a separate customer decision for the AED 300 request | Trust Passport → Quote Lock |
| Unclear repair progress | Six stage timeline and sample notes | Shows what a customer should expect during a repair | Quote Lock → Tracking |
| Lost repair paperwork | Invoice, approval history and warranty reminder | Keeps the final scope and authorized total available for review | My Garage → Repair detail |
| Time spent visiting garages | Comparable fictional profiles in one place | Demonstrates a future comparison journey on one phone | Garages tab |

The prototype does not verify real garages, market prices, diagnostic accuracy or repair quality. Quote Lock does not enforce garage behavior.
